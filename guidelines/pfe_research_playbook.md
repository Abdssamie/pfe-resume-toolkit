# PFE & Internship Research Strategy Playbook 🎯

This playbook outlines proven strategies for Moroccan and international students seeking high-value **Projet de Fin d'Études (PFE)** or pre-employment internships.

---

## 1. The PFE Opportunity Landscape

Companies recruit PFE interns across three primary tiers:

1. **Multinationals & Industrial Leaders (e.g., Stellantis, Renault, Gotion, JESA, STMicroelectronics)**:
   - High competition, strict formal processes, compliance requirements.
   - Look for candidates who demonstrate discipline, familiarity with standards (ISO, NM, Six Sigma, safety), and clear communication.
2. **Consulting, Digital Staffing & IT Services (e.g., ValueIT, Capgemini, DXC, Devoteam, SQLI)**:
   - Value immediate technical autonomy, adaptability to client tech stacks, and quick problem-solving.
   - Look for candidates with tangible portfolios, live demos, and open-source contributions.
3. **High-Growth Startups & Scale-ups**:
   - Value end-to-end builders who can own a feature from database to UI.
   - Faster response times; reach out directly to the CTO or Engineering Lead.

---

## 2. The Multi-Channel Research Strategy

Don't rely solely on job boards where hundreds of applicants submit generic resumes.

### Channel 1: The Direct Recruiter Search (LinkedIn)
1. Use exact search queries on LinkedIn:
   - `"PFE" AND "Stage" AND ("Kénitra" OR "Casablanca" OR "Rabat")`
   - `"Recrutement" AND "PFE 2026" OR "Sujet PFE"`
   - Filter by **Posts** posted in the **Past week**.
2. Identify the specific Talent Acquisition specialist or HR recruiter who made the post.
3. Send a tailored message directly instead of clicking "Easy Apply".

### Channel 2: The Direct Engineering Lead Outreach
- Identify the Lead Engineer, Technical Project Manager, or Plant Quality Manager at your target company.
- Reach out with a short, respectful inquiry demonstrating knowledge of their tech stack or operational challenges.

---

## 3. The "Smart Fallback" Tactic

When an application asks for fresh graduates or experienced candidates, don't disqualify yourself immediately if you are still a final-year student:

- **The Primary Target**: Apply confidently for the flagship role (e.g., *Quality Engineer* or *Software Engineer*).
- **The Body Fallback**: In the body of the cover letter or email (never in the subject line), express enthusiastic openness to a *Management Trainee*, *Graduate Program*, or *PFE Intern* track if the primary role requires prior graduation.
- **Why this works**: Recruiters often have budget for multiple tracks and appreciate self-awareness and eagerness.

---

## 4. Email Outreach & Scheduling Optimization

Timing significantly impacts whether your email gets opened or buried:

- **Best Days to Send**: **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**.
  - Avoid Mondays (inboxes are overwhelmed with weekend backlog).
  - Avoid Friday afternoons (recruiters wrap up the week).
- **Best Times to Schedule**:
  - **Slot 1 (Prime)**: **08:30 AM – 09:30 AM** (catches the recruiter right as they sit down with morning coffee).
  - **Slot 2 (Midday)**: **01:30 PM – 02:15 PM** (right after lunch break).
- **Subject Line Discipline**:
  - Keep subjects concise and professional.
  - Include school/university name if the recruiter specifically targeted that institution:
    `Candidature – Quality Engineer – Université Ibn Tofail – Prénom NOM`
  - For IT/tech roles:
    `Candidature – Stage PFE / Développeur Full-Stack – Prénom NOM`
